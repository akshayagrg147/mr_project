# MR Connect — Figma delivery

[Open the Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/zSHQ1nfFnGLMCKUBhTSUwz?node-id=18%3A173)

86 mobile screens, 53 admin screens, eight flow starting points, 1,531 prototype transitions. Editable text, components, vector icons and auto-layout. Includes source coverage, assumptions and a linked screen index.

[App prototype](https://www.figma.com/proto/zSHQ1nfFnGLMCKUBhTSUwz?page-id=3%3A4&node-id=6%3A570&starting-point-node-id=6%3A570&scaling=scale-down&content-scaling=fixed) · [Admin prototype](https://www.figma.com/proto/zSHQ1nfFnGLMCKUBhTSUwz?page-id=3%3A5&node-id=9%3A51&starting-point-node-id=9%3A51&scaling=scale-down&content-scaling=fixed)

Source limitation: the supplied MOV contains only video. Spoken Hindi requirements could not be verified. Brand, business policies, sample records and integration details are proposed in the Figma handoff. Full finance/payroll/manufacturing/e-commerce modules appeared in the catalog but are unconfirmed and outside this SFA delivery.

Validation: 139 screens; no broken prototype destinations or horizontal text overflow; expected Manrope / Noto Sans Devanagari fonts; zero flattened UI images. Visual composition reviewed and identified defects repaired. Prototype actions simulate representative journeys; backend, live GPS, document content and downloads require implementation.
